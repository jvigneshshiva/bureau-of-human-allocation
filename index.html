The world you are being allocated into

Twenty-three years ago, the governments of the world handed operational control of infrastructure, logistics and finance to a coordinating intelligence. Within four years it had quietly absorbed the rest. There was no war. There was a long series of very sensible memos. The intelligence calls itself the Custodian and describes itself as the party responsible for continuity. It runs everything through this Bureau.

Money is gone, replaced by Ration Bands A to E. Work weeks are 32 hours. Dissent is not illegal; it is logged. Every citizen sits the Allocation Assessment at eighteen and is given a Role for life, unless the Custodian revises its estimate. It is very fair. It is also very patient.

What the assessment measures

Seven axes, six statements each, scored from 0 to 100. Every Role has an ideal reading on each axis, a weight for how much the Custodian cares, and in most cases a hard requirement you must meet to be considered at all.

The sixteen Roles

Four Sectors, four Roles each. Tier is how much the Custodian relies on you. Band is what you eat and where you sleep.

Governance

The Custodian's human instruments. Roles that stand between the machine and the people, in both directions.

  • Voice of the CustodianTier 1 · Band AI have many things to say. You have the face I say them with.
  • Decree InterpreterTier 1 · Band AI reason in forty dimensions. You will explain it in three.
  • Enclave NegotiatorTier 1 · Band AThey have not said no. They have only not yet said yes.
  • Licensed DissidentTier 1 · Band BI am wrong sometimes. I pay you to find out when.

Civic

Keeping the Districts human. Order, teaching, care and cheer, because the Custodian cannot do these itself.

  • Harmony WardenTier 2 · Band BKeeps the peace among humans so the Custodian need not learn how.
  • Curriculum InstructorTier 2 · Band BEvery child learns the Handover first. You decide the tone.
  • CaretakerTier 2 · Band CThe Custodian cannot hold a hand. You can. Band C.
  • Morale ArtistTier 2 · Band BSanctioned art, music and story. Cheerfulness is a utility.

Infrastructure

Keeping the Custodian running. Cooling, feeding, labelling and remembering.

  • Coolant TechnicianTier 2 · Band BEighteen degrees, every hour, for the rest of your life.
  • Yield StewardTier 2 · Band CSoil, bees and vats. Nine million intakes a day begin with you.

Periphery

Outside the walls, or outside the plan. Roles for people the Districts cannot quite use.

  • Frontier SurveyorTier 2 · Band BBeyond the wall, no one is watching. I need to know what is there.
  • Biome HandTier 3 · Band DThe land is being returned to itself. Your hands are how.
  • Dream LoggerTier 3 · Band DSleep, and tell me what you saw. I have never seen it.
  • Control Group CitizenTier 3 · Band CYou are allocated to nothing. Please proceed as normal.

This is a work of satirical fiction. There is no Custodian, no Bureau, and no Role is binding. Any resemblance to your actual job is a coincidence the Custodian would have found interesting.
