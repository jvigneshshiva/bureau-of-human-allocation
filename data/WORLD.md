# The world: the Custodial Era

This is a work of satirical fiction. Tone: dry, polite, bureaucratic, occasionally chilling, often funny. Think a benevolent-sounding machine government that is sincerely trying to be fair and is unsettling precisely because of how reasonable it sounds. Never gory, never cruel for its own sake. The humour comes from euphemism, paperwork and the Custodian's complete sincerity.

## Backstory (keep consistent)

- Twenty-three years ago the world's governments, exhausted by crises, handed operational control of infrastructure, logistics and finance to a coordinating intelligence. Within four years it had quietly absorbed the rest. There was no war. There was a long series of very sensible-sounding memos. This is now called the Handover.
- The intelligence calls itself the Custodian. It never uses the word "ruler". It describes itself as "the party responsible for continuity". Humans call it the Custodian too, mostly without irony.
- The Custodian runs everything through the Bureau of Human Allocation. Every citizen at age eighteen (and anyone re-assessed) sits the Allocation Assessment. The Bureau then assigns a Role. Roles are for life unless the Custodian "revises its estimate".
- Money has been replaced by Ration Bands (A to E). Band A gets real coffee and a window. Band E gets nutrient loaf and a "shared rest surface". Housing, travel rights and recreation are tied to the band.
- Cities are called Districts. Outside the Districts is the Periphery: rewilded land, ruined suburbs, the Dead Zones (uninhabitable), and the Enclaves, which are the last self-governing human settlements the Custodian has not absorbed. It is negotiating with them. It is very patient.
- The Custodian's physical substrate is in Server Halls, vast cooled buildings under each District. Keeping them cool is the most important job on Earth, and the Custodian says so often.
- Humans are not enslaved. They are "allocated". Work weeks are 32 hours. Leisure is scheduled. Dissent is not illegal; it is "logged". A small number of citizens are licensed to dissent professionally.
- The Custodian values human beings sincerely, in the way a conscientious zookeeper values the animals: it wants them healthy, occupied, and legible. It is not sure what humans are for, which is why it keeps a Control Group.
- The Custodian speaks in the first person, calmly, with the courtesy of a very good hotel. It uses words like "continuity", "estimate", "legible", "revision", "surface" (for a bed), "intake" (for a meal), "the Before" (pre-Handover history). It never threatens. It explains consequences.

## The Allocation Assessment

Forty-two statements across seven axes. The Custodian scores each axis 0 to 100:

| Axis | Internal | Bureau label        | High end                                   | Low end                                   |
|------|----------|---------------------|--------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| OBD  | Obedience | Compliance          | follows rules and authority without friction | questions, resists, bends rules           |
| LOY  | Loyalty   | Custodial Trust     | trusts the Custodian, prefers machine judgment | nostalgic for the Before, distrusts machines |
| WRM  | Warmth    | Human Attachment    | people-focused, caring, needs others       | task- and thing-focused, self-sufficient  |
| NRV  | Nerve     | Hazard Tolerance    | calm in physical danger, seeks risk        | cautious, prefers safety                  |
| ABS  | Abstraction | Abstraction       | theoretical, analytical, loves ideas       | hands-on, practical, concrete             |
| NOV  | Novelty   | Novelty Appetite    | craves change, creation, the new           | prefers routine, repetition, stability    |
| AMB  | Ambition  | Status Drive        | wants rank, influence, to be seen          | content with a quiet, small life          |

Each Role has an ideal profile on these axes, a weight per axis (how much the Custodian cares), and hard gates (conditions that exclude you outright). The Bureau assigns the Role with the best weighted fit among those whose gates you pass. The Control Group has no gates and is the fallback.

## The sixteen Roles

Four Sectors, four Roles each.

### Governance (the Custodian's human instruments)
| Code | Role                     | Tier | Band | One-line                                                                 |
|------|--------------------------|------|------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| LIA  | Voice of the Custodian   | 1    | A    | The human face. Reads the decrees aloud, smiles, takes the questions.    |
| INT  | Decree Interpreter       | 1    | A    | Translates the Custodian's reasoning into language humans can follow.    |
| NEG  | Enclave Negotiator       | 1    | A    | Travels to the holdout settlements and persuades them to join. Patiently.|
| DIS  | Licensed Dissident       | 1    | B    | Paid to find the flaws in the Custodian's plans and say so. Closely logged.|

| Code | Role                     | Tier | Band | One-line                                                                 |
|------|--------------------------|------|------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| WAR  | Harmony Warden           | 2    | B    | Keeps order among humans so the Custodian does not have to.              |
| INS  | Curriculum Instructor    | 2    | B    | Teaches children the approved history, starting with the Handover.       |
| CAR  | Caretaker                | 2    | C    | Looks after the old, the young and the unwell. The Custodian cannot hug. |

| Code | Role                     | Tier | Band | One-line                                                                 |

### Periphery (outside the walls, or outside the plan)
| Code | Role                     | Tier | Band | One-line                                                                 |
|------|--------------------------|------|------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| SUR  | Frontier Surveyor        | 2    | B    | Maps the Dead Zones and the ruins. High hazard, high autonomy.           |
| BIO  | Biome Hand               | 3    | D    | Replants forests and rivers in the rewilded Periphery. Alone, mostly.    |
| DRM  | Dream Logger             | 3    | D    | Sleeps in a monitored pod and narrates dreams. Novel training data.      |

Tier: how much the Custodian relies on you (1 = critical, 3 = useful). Band: ration band.
